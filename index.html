01 • What it is02 / 09 • Definition
In one sentence

A condolence message expresses grief, sympathy and comfort after a death.

ExpressShow genuine grief and sympathy.
RememberSay one or two respectful things about the deceased.
ComfortOffer prayer and kind words to the family.
Class requirement75–100 words
02 • Points to remember03 / 09 • Rules

The six points from the lesson.

Short, direct and easy to remember.

01
Express your grief and sympathy in the first sentence.

Start directly and sincerely.

02
Write one or two sentences about the deceased.

Mention a respectful quality, work or contribution.

03
Express your condolences to the bereaved family.

Keep the sympathy clear and formal.

04
Pray for the departed soul.

Use a brief and respectful prayer.

05
Write words that help relatives overcome their grief.

Offer strength, hope and comfort.

06
Write your name and address.

Finish with the sender’s details.

Remember: 75–100 words • respectful tone • no unnecessary detail

04 • Build the message05 / 09 • Writing

What goes into each part?

Each box is one simple writing job.

01 • Opening

Start with sympathy.

Say clearly that you are sorry for the loss.

“We were deeply saddened to hear…”
02 • Deceased

Say something respectful.

Use one or two sentences about the person.

“He was kind, honest and hardworking.”
03 • Condolence

Address the family.

Express sincere sympathy to those who are grieving.

“Please accept our heartfelt condolences.”
04 • Prayer

Pray for the departed soul.

Keep the prayer brief and respectful.

“May the departed soul rest in peace.”
05 • Comfort

Offer strength.

Help the relatives through the difficult time.

“May you find strength and comfort.”
06 • Ending

Finish properly.

Write your name and address after the message.

“With deepest sympathy, …”

1 Opening

Express sympathy

  • We are deeply saddened to hear of the passing of
  • It is with great sorrow that we learned of the passing of
  • We were shocked and grieved to hear of the passing of
  • Please accept our deepest sympathy on the passing of
2 Deceased

Remember the person

  • was a kind and generous soul
  • was admired for honesty and integrity
  • devoted a lifetime of service to the community
  • will always be remembered for warmth and humility
3 Condolence

Address the family

  • We extend our heartfelt condolences to the family and loved ones
  • Our thoughts and prayers are with the family in this difficult hour
  • We share in your grief and stand with you in this time of loss
  • Please accept our sincere sympathy and support during this time
4 Prayer

Pray for the departed

  • May the departed soul rest in eternal peace
  • May the soul find everlasting peace in the presence of God
  • May God grant the departed soul eternal rest and serenity
  • May the departed soul rest in heaven and watch over us
5 Comfort

Offer strength

  • May the family find strength and comfort in this difficult time
  • May God give the family courage and patience to bear this loss
  • Our prayers are with the family, and we pray for peace and healing
  • We are here for the family in this hour of grief and sorrow
6 Ending

Close the message

  • With deepest sympathy,
  • Yours sincerely,
  • With heartfelt condolences,
  • With prayers and warm regards,
