02 • Points to remember03 / 08

The six points from the lesson.

Short, direct and easy to remember.

01
Express your grief and sympathy in the first sentence.

Start directly and sincerely.

02
Write one or two sentences about the deceased.

Mention a respectful quality, work or contribution.

03
Express your condolences to the bereaved family.

Keep the sympathy clear and formal.

04
Pray for the departed soul.

Use a brief and respectful prayer.

05
Write words that help relatives overcome their grief.

Offer strength, hope and comfort.

06
Write your name and address.

Finish with the sender’s details.

Remember: 75–100 words • respectful tone • no unnecessary detail

04 • Build the message05 / 08

What goes into each part?

Each box is one simple writing job.

01 • Opening

Start with sympathy.

Say clearly that you are sorry for the loss.

“We were deeply saddened to hear…”
02 • Deceased

Say something respectful.

Use one or two sentences about the person.

“He was kind, honest and hardworking.”
03 • Condolence

Address the family.

Express sincere sympathy to those who are grieving.

“Please accept our heartfelt condolences.”
04 • Prayer

Pray for the departed soul.

Keep the prayer brief and respectful.

“May the departed soul rest in peace.”
05 • Comfort

Offer strength.

Help the relatives through the difficult time.

“May you find strength and comfort.”
06 • Ending

Finish properly.

Write your name and address after the message.

“With deepest sympathy, …”
